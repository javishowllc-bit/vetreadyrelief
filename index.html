The solution: Tell it once. We do the legwork.

Free. It does the hunt, the paperwork, and the follow-up. Onboarding is three steps.

  1. Upload your DD-214

    Your SSN is auto-redacted except the last 4 digits.

    We match programs near you and search live if needed.

  3. Sign your hardship

Help we cover

Vet Ready Relief is built to cover every kind of help for any veteran. It is free, and it is always available to everyone.

  • Emergency cash and relief funds

    Find and apply to emergency relief sources that can help while a VA claim is pending.

  • Housing

    Help with rent, deposits, and other housing support so a family can stay housed.

  • Utilities

    Programs that can keep the lights, gas, water, and phone on while you wait.

  • Food

    Food assistance and related programs for veterans and their families.

  • Student aid

    Education and student aid options beyond the VA claim or benefit decision you are waiting on.

  • Pet care and service dogs

    Low-cost vaccines, flea treatment, and spay or neuter help, including programs such as HEART, Dogs on Deployment Operation Pet Wellness, and OC Animal Allies, plus support for service dogs.

What the app does for you

  • Every source, open or closed.

    Eligibility is flagged. Spouse programs are included.

  • Your documents, kept safe.

    Your DD-214 and other papers are stored once in a secure account protected with 2-factor sign-in.

  • Forms pre-filled for you.

    With your consent, your documents and signed hardship statement fill in each application.

  • You review, sign, we submit.

    Nothing goes out until you approve it.

  • Calendar and alerts.

    Deadlines, appointments, and a notice when a case is approved, declined, or changes.

  • Need help with your VA claim?

    With your OK, we connect you to an accredited VSO. We never file VA claims ourselves.
